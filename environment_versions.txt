Reproducibility note
--------------------
Seeds are fixed in the experiment scripts, and the Optuna TPE samplers use
fixed sampler seeds. Exact floating-point equality across GPU architectures,
CUDA libraries, and PyTorch builds is not guaranteed for neural-network
training. The separately supplied intermediary outputs permit exact
regeneration of the paper's downstream tables and figures without retraining.
